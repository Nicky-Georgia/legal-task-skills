## Requirements from the PDF

- Page 1: read both review tabs and consider their feedback.
- Page 2: repair the assignment prompt and rubric so they are accurate, clear,
  and consistent with the task requirements.
- Pages 3–4: revise reference answers and matter documents substantively in Word.
  Remove generic, repetitive, inflated, and awkward prose and unnatural document
  organization. The result should display a lawyer's reasoning and precision.
- Page 4: corrected files are intended for the project's Final Submission tab.
  The suggested handling time is 3–5 hours, depending on scope; examples range
  from 1–50 matter files and 5–25 reference pages. These are planning examples,
  not fixed limits, a minimum time expenditure, or metadata values.
- Pages 5–7: replace lists of unexplained commands with usable legal advice.
  Apply law to concrete facts, identify actual sources, address meaningful
  opposing arguments, calibrate confidence, and recommend specific next steps.
  Remove surplus bulleting, bold text, boilerplate headings, repetitious legal
  propositions, and theatrical emphasis when they impair the work product.
- Pages 7–8: matter files should reflect their business function and speaker.
  Look for repeated exposition, identical voices across supposed authors,
  gratuitous admissions of legal liability, implausibly complete narratives,
  vague actors, and memo-like headings in routine emails.
