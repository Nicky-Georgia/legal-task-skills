# Professional legal drafting pipeline

Use this sequence: determine the document's purpose; repair substance against
the record; edit prose without changing legal effect; verify sources; check the
final Word layout. Correct substance before polishing it.

## Choose the mode

### Neutral advice and research memoranda

Give the reader a supported answer and its practical consequences early. Develop
the controlling rule, relevant facts, application, competing position, and
recommendation in connected prose. Preserve uncertainty where it affects the
decision. A short answer should not promise a result the analysis cannot sustain.
Retain conventional sections where useful; do not force every memo into a fixed
outline or turn risk advice into advocacy.

Explain which facts change the outcome and why. Replace generic reminders to
research, consult, verify, or consider issues with the actual analysis or a
specific unresolved question and its consequence. A task list is appropriate
for next steps, not as a substitute for the requested reasoning.

#### Advice to a supervising partner or board

- Write from the associate's advisory role. State the recommendation and the
  reason for it in connected prose. Identify who would act where responsibility
  matters and preserve material conditions and timing. Reserve mandatory
  language for legal or contractual duties. A succession of “Counsel should”
  sentences can read like a task list even after bare commands are removed.
- If the assignment calls for a short decision note, give the reader the choice,
  recommendation, reason, any time-critical steps and the fallback. The analysis
  that follows supplies the law, application and competing position needed to
  support that decision. Repeat a deadline or other fact only where the note
  must stand alone or a distinct part of the analysis depends on it. Keep any
  requested operative instructions or checklist clearly identified as such.
- Check what each requested statement would attest to, who could make it, the
  applicable standard, and what evidence or inquiry would support it. Use the
  source's precise terminology. Distinguish authenticating a record or copy,
  attesting to issuance or service, and certifying substantive facts. A copy
  foundation or issuer declaration supports only what it actually establishes.
  Identify the missing foundation, who can supply it, and what may proceed
  if it remains unavailable.
- Put a material, time-sensitive record defect near the decision it affects.
  Use a short descriptive heading and state the practical cure and protective
  fallback. Use the document's existing emphasis conventions or a requested
  house style. Move technical detail that does not affect the immediate choice
  to the relevant analysis.

### Advocacy, submissions, and persuasive correspondence

Organize around the relief sought and the legal path to obtaining it. Use precise
headings and factual application. Address the strongest material opposing point
and relevant adverse authority fairly. Match certainty to evidence. Retain
procedural and factual context the forum needs; avoid manufactured drama,
personal attacks, unsupported absolutes, and rhetorical claims of inevitability.

Use the task's actual jurisdiction and forum requirements. A U.S. writing source
does not make U.S. doctrine, citation style, or procedure applicable elsewhere.

### Contracts and other operative instruments

Begin with the supplied agreement and any approved precedent or instructions.
Compare the proposed change with the agreed commercial position. Check the
responsible party, obligation or permission, object, trigger, deadline, conditions,
exceptions, remedy, and interaction with other clauses before and after editing.

Preserve defined terms, cross-references, risk allocation, limitation carve-outs,
notice mechanics, consent requirements, survival, and precedence provisions.
Distinguish a drafting defect from an unfavorable but agreed term. Do not invent
an approved playbook or silently substitute a standard form. Do not collapse
“indemnify and hold harmless,” change “shall” mechanically, equate different
efforts standards, or change conjunctions just to simplify prose.

An approved playbook can supply positions when actually provided. Otherwise
check against the task's supported deal terms and flag choices those terms do
not resolve. OpenAgreements is an optional source for a specifically requested
new standard form, not the default engine for revising an existing task pack.

### Matter emails, notes, and business records

Write for the sender's actual recipient and immediate purpose. Preserve the
sender's contemporaneous knowledge, authority, uncertainty, and distinct voice.
Do not make non-lawyers sound like a memorandum or give every email the whole
case history. Keep authentic shorthand where intelligible; remove compressed
commands where the recipient cannot tell who must act, when, and on what terms.
Do not turn uncertain observations into admissions or remove existing admissions.

## Restrained prose pass

Cut filler, repetition, vague attribution, ornamental transitions, inflated
adjectives, and narration about how the argument is organized. Name the relevant
actor, provision, event, and consequence when the sources permit. Join ideas in
paragraphs that actually explain their relationship. Use bullets and tables for
parallel items or comparisons, with enough prose for material legal reasoning.

Preserve quotations, terms of art, burdens, negative propositions, conditions,
exceptions, degrees of certainty, and the reach of modifiers. Do not impose
blanket bans on punctuation, passive voice, headings, or familiar legal phrases.
Do not remove a useful qualification because it sounds cautious. Avoid routine
global replacement and wholesale restyling of an otherwise usable document.

This pass is a constrained legal edit, not a general humanizer rewrite. The
separate `humanizer` skill may help with the change overview, but is not needed
to edit operative clauses or decide law.

## Final reader pass for memoranda

Read the heading outline and the full draft from the intended reader's
perspective. Remove sections that repeat a recommendation or factual account
already given where it matters. Cut redundant background, boilerplate
transitions, unsupported abstractions and conclusions that merely restate the
opening. A long memo may lose entire sections. Use a clean redraft when that is
faster and gives the advice a coherent structure.

Read the surviving prose for bare commands, serial “should” clauses,
research-to-self notes, unresolved source markers and editorial directions.
Delete internal residue. Where a proposed action matters to the client,
explain why, who would take it and any decisive condition or deadline. For
example, replace “Verify notice. Calculate cure. Revisit termination.”
with “I would defer termination until we have proof of receipt, which starts
the contractual cure period.” Keep the material rule, application,
counterargument, qualification and source support after every cut. Deliver a
clean copy.

## Source verification pass

For each material legal proposition in the final reference, associate the claim
with the actual source and location inspected. For each citation, check identity,
court/jurisdiction, date, rule or holding, procedural context, quotation, pinpoint,
and whether the sentence fairly characterizes the source. Resolve short forms
and distinguish holdings from dicta, party submissions, and summaries.

Read the relevant primary text in context. A search snippet, digest, title, or
case-existence result does not prove support. Check legal currency to the task's
as-of date when material; do not import later law. Distinguish source support
from later-history/citator verification and state any limits in the overview.

Keep a compact scratch record of proposition, source, locator, support status,
and action taken. Missing support is not cured by a plausible invented citation.
Correct an overstatement only to the extent evidence supports the narrower
claim. If a necessary authority cannot be verified, preserve the issue for the
user rather than quietly labeling the document fully verified.

## Selection and attribution

Public instructions inspected on 15 November 2026 informed this original,
task-specific adaptation. The external skills are not installed or executed,
and their commands, mandatory outputs, and approval procedures are not imported.

- [LegalQuants Litigation Writing](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/blob/main/skills/litigation/writing/SKILL.md):
  selected for purpose-based routing, record fidelity, and the distinction between
  advocacy and neutral advice.
- [LegalQuants Cite Check](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/blob/main/skills/litigation/cite-check/SKILL.md):
  selected for checking a proposition, quotation, and pinpoint against full source
  text. This skill uses a local verification pass, not its separate-session runner
  or mandatory HTML report.
- [Persuasive Legal Writing, Larissa Meredith-Flister](https://app.decimal.ai/s/lawve-ai-persuasive-legal-writing/SKILL.md):
  selected for identifying prose that narrates its own argument and relies on
  empty abstractions. Aggressive framing and emotional rhetoric are not defaults.
- [LegalQuants Playbook Review](https://github.com/LegalQuants/lq-plugin-oss/blob/main/skills/transactional/playbook-review/SKILL.md):
  informs clause-to-approved-position checks; the external skill requires its own
  approved playbook package and returns an issues list, so it is not a direct
  substitute for the clean Word editing requested here.
- [Deslop](https://github.com/fayerman-source/deslop/blob/main/SKILL.md) and
  [OpenAgreements](https://github.com/open-agreements/open-agreements/blob/main/skills/agreements/open-agreements/SKILL.md)
  were considered. Blanket lexical/typographic replacements and standard-form
  generation are unsuitable defaults for targeted preservation of existing files.
